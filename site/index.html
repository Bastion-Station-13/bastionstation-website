What is Space Station 13?

SS13 is a round-based roleplaying game set on a space station that is trying to kill you. It has robust sandbox features and every part of the world is interactable. Your round is what you make it, and no two rounds are the same - every player has a job, but your goals are your own to set. Expect comedy, drama, and lots of explosions.

Bastion is a fork of MonkeStation 2.0 (itself built on /tg/station), with scheduled playtests, a unique ruleset, and a focus on rounds curated by Game Masters, like D&D.
